NeRF-DA combines radiance-field deblurring with active learning to improve training-data selection for sharp novel-view synthesis. The seminar reviewed its motivation, related research, framework, working principle, and the authors' reported results.\cite{bib1}

The key takeaway is that useful viewpoint coverage and informative images matter alongside dataset size. The reported improvements should be interpreted within the evaluated scenes and metrics. Camera-pose dependence, selection trade-offs, and computational cost remain relevant considerations for broader use.
