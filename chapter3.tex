\section{Problem Addressed}
How can a radiance-field model reconstruct sharp novel views from a collection of blurred images while avoiding redundant or uninformative training samples and addressing uneven viewpoint coverage?\cite{bib1}
\section{Significance of the Problem}
Using more blurred images does not necessarily improve reconstruction. Severe blur and insufficient information near the edges of the captured light field can reduce the value of the training data. Selecting useful views is therefore central to the problem addressed by NeRF-DA.
\section{Scope of the Seminar}
The seminar examines the selected paper's objectives, framework, working principle, reported results, applications, limitations, and possible research extensions. Numerical results reproduced in this report belong to the paper's authors.
