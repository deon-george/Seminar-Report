\section{{Potential Practical Uses}}

\subsection{{Personalized Rehabilitation Planning}}

Predicted outcomes and explanations may help clinicians understand differences in recovery potential and consider patient-specific rehabilitation goals.

\subsection{{Treatment-Response Assessment}}

Classification models may help researchers investigate which patients are more likely to meet the response criterion defined for a particular treatment study.

\subsection{{Clinical Decision Support}}

Interpretable predictions may provide additional information for clinicians reviewing assessments and planning rehabilitation. Predictions should complement, not replace, clinical evaluation.

\subsection{{Rehabilitation Research}}

The framework can help researchers investigate relationships between baseline motor function, spasticity, balance, and post-treatment outcomes.

\subsection{{Explainable Medical AI}}

The combination of machine learning and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) illustrates how medical prediction systems can be evaluated by numerical performance and transparency.

These are potential uses; the paper does not establish that a fully deployed clinical product has been validated for routine care.
