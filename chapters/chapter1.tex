\section{\MakeUppercase{Overview of Novel View Synthesis}}

Novel view synthesis is a key area in computer vision that focuses on generating images of a scene from new, unseen camera viewpoints using a set of existing images captured from multiple angles. This technology is crucial for creating immersive experiences and realistic simulations. To achieve this, various methods have been explored, ranging from explicit methods like point clouds, voxel grids, and depth maps, to more recent implicit representation methods which have shown significant advantages in producing high-quality results.\cite{bib5}


\section{Motivation and Importance}
Real-world multi-view images may contain motion blur, defocus blur, redundant viewpoints, and uneven spatial coverage. These issues can reduce the quality of reconstructed scenes. The selected paper by Hong and Kim introduces NeRF-DA, combining deblurring and active learning to improve the selection of training data. This seminar reviews the authors' approach and reported evidence; it does not claim a new implementation or experimental contribution.\cite{bib1}
