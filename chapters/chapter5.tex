\section{Selected Paper}
S. Hong and E. Kim, ``NeRF-DA: Neural Radiance Fields Deblurring With Active Learning,'' \textit{IEEE Signal Processing Letters}, vol. 32, pp. 261--265, 2025, doi: 10.1109/LSP.2024.3511350.\cite{bib1}
\section{Objectives}
The authors aim to improve sharp novel-view reconstruction from blurred, imbalanced multi-view images through selective training data and active learning.
\section{Architecture}
The framework combines camera-pose analysis, image-entropy-based selection, a Deblur-NeRF model containing a deformable sparse kernel and a radiance field, and an iterative reconstruction-and-selection loop. The framework diagram and detailed steps are presented in the next chapter.
\section{Key Contributions of the Authors}
\begin{itemize}
\item Selection of an initial training subset using camera position and image information.
\item Inclusion of additional camera poses to address limited viewpoint coverage.
\item Reconstruction and selection of candidate views for subsequent NeRF training.
\end{itemize}
These contributions are those of Hong and Kim; the student's contribution is the seminar review and critical discussion.\cite{bib1}
