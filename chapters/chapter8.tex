\section{Potential Practical Uses}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Augmented and virtual reality:} reconstruction of scenes for immersive viewing from imperfect image captures.
\item \textbf{Robotics:} improved visual representations of an environment captured by moving cameras.
\item \textbf{Autonomous driving:} potential support for reconstructing road scenes affected by camera motion blur.
\item \textbf{Digital documentation:} novel-view visualization of objects, indoor spaces, and heritage scenes.
\end{itemize}
These are potential uses of sharp scene reconstruction. The supplied report does not establish deployed systems or application-specific performance guarantees.
