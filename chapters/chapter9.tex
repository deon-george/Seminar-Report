\chapter{\MakeUppercase{Advantages and Limitations}}
\setcounter{equation}{0}

\section{Strengths Discussed in the Report}
\begin{itemize}
\item Selective data curation reduces the influence of redundant and severely blurred views.
\item Joint deblurring and scene reconstruction uses information from multiple views.
\item The reported comparisons use PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS to assess reconstruction quality.
\end{itemize}
\section{Limitations Identified through Critical Analysis}
The following points are seminar-level analysis and should not be presented as explicit author claims without checking the paper.
\begin{itemize}
\item Reconstruction depends on camera-pose accuracy and adequate scene coverage.
\item Too few selected views can remove information required for reliable reconstruction.
\item Image entropy is an information proxy; noise and texture may affect its suitability as a sharpness indicator.
\item Iterative reconstruction and retraining introduce computational cost.
\item The reported datasets do not establish performance for every dynamic, low-light, or safety-critical application.
\end{itemize}
